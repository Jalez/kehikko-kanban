- **Notes about the epic you are on.** Write a short note in Kanban and it is kept with the project, under the parts of the epic you had picked.
- **Follows the parts you pick.** Pick parts in Kehikot's bar and Kanban shows only the notes written under them.
- **Open notes in your Inbox.** Every open note of the epic waits in Kehikot's Inbox until you tick it off.
- **Agents can read them.** An agent on the kehikko can read your notes, and `/kanban` asks one for what is still open; only you add, finish or remove a note.
